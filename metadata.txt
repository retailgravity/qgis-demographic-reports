about=This plugin generates professional demographic reports and datafills from US Census block group data. It uses proportional area allocation for accurate spatial aggregation. Supports multiple data package tiers (Basic, Expanded, Professional). Features include point-based reports with customizable radius, polygon datafill for custom geographies, PDF report generation with custom branding, CSV export with metadata, and calculated fields (densities, weighted averages, growth rates). Ideal for demographic analysis, market research, and urban planning.
